Delete Your Renta Account

Renta allows users to permanently delete their account and associated account data directly from the Renta mobile application.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Renta app.
  2. Go to your Profile.
  3. Open Account Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Follow the confirmation steps shown in the app.
Important: Account deletion is permanent. Once the deletion is confirmed, your account and applicable associated data will be deleted according to Renta's data retention and legal requirements.

Data deleted

When you delete your Renta account, Renta deletes the personal account data associated with the account, subject to any information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate obligations.

Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period when required by law, necessary to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, enforce agreements, or maintain security. Any retained information will be handled according to Renta's privacy policy.

Need help?

If you cannot access your account or cannot complete the deletion process in the app, please contact Renta support.
